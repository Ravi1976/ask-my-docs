# Sample Company Leave Policy (fictional)

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Unused leave up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next year. Leave beyond 10 days expires on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required
if sick leave lasts more than 3 consecutive days.

## Work from home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Requests must be raised in the HR portal at least one day in advance.

## Notice period
The standard notice period is 60 days for managers and 30 days for other employees.
